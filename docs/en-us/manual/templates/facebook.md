# Facebook: page post, group rules, moderation and campaign (short form)

One section per item. Use `TODO:` for undefined fields. Never record personal data of members or commenters, real page, group or ad-account IDs, or tokens. A person always executes, after approval.

- **Page post:** topic, goal, official destination link, planned date, status, publisher, text, image alt text.
- **Group rules:** purpose, at most eight rules, each with a verifiable criterion and a consequence decided by a person.
- **Moderation plan:** trigger, category (spam, offence, question, complaint, report), suggested action, message template, decision owner; plus a log. No removal, ban or deletion without recorded human decision.
- **Ad campaign:** goal, period, budget (approved by an authorised person), aggregate audience with no sensitive personal attributes, creative and alt text, advertising and partnership disclosure.
- **Compliance checklist:** Meta Community Standards and Advertising Standards checked on the review date; no misleading claims; third-party licences; no personal data; no tokens or real IDs; human approval recorded.
