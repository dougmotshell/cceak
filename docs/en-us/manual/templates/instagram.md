# Instagram: editorial calendar and post sheet (short form)

One sheet per post; the calendar lives in its own file. Use `TODO:` for missing data. Never record personal data, tokens or real account IDs.

- **Calendar:** period, pillars, approver, and the person who publishes (always a human). One row per post: date, format (feed, carousel, reels, stories), topic, pillar, status, publisher.
- **Post sheet:** topic, format, planned date, status (`draft`, `awaiting approval`, `approved`, `published manually`, `cancelled`), publisher.
- **Sections:** caption (hook first); alt text per image; video caption for reels and videos; hashtags (few and relevant).
- **Compliance checklist:** third-party music, image and font licences; advertising disclosure (Instagram rules and CONAR); no unproven promises; no personal or customer data; no tokens or private links; platform limits checked against Meta's official documentation; `node scripts/social-copy-check.mjs` returns 0; human approval recorded.

The full Portuguese template is the source of truth: `docs/pt-br/manual/templates/instagram.md`.
